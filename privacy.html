Privacy Policy

Hungry Birds Hotel & Resort values your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with our website, make a booking, or communicate with our team.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, and nationality.
  • Booking information including check-in and check-out dates, room type, number of guests, and special requests.
  • Payment information required to process reservations, where applicable.
  • Technical information such as browser type, IP address, and browsing activity on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data to:

  • Process and confirm reservations and related services.
  • Communicate with you about bookings, confirmations, and customer support.
  • Improve the quality of our hospitality services and guest experience.
  • Send promotional offers, newsletters, and updates when you have consented to receive them.
  • Comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with trusted third parties where necessary to provide services such as payment processing, reservation systems, and local authorities when required by law.

4. Cookies and Website Analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools to understand usage patterns, improve website performance, and personalize guest experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, although some features of our website may not function optimally.

5. Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet-based system is completely secure, and we encourage guests to protect their own credentials.

6. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as required to fulfill business needs, meet legal requirements, or resolve disputes. When no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, correct inaccurate information, object to certain processing, or request deletion in accordance with applicable law. Please contact us if you wish to exercise these rights.
